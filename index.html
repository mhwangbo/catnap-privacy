CATNAP Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Applies to the CATNAP Android app (package com.catnap.game)

CATNAP is a puzzle game about tucking sleepy cats into a magical bag. This policy explains what the game stores and what it does not do. In short: the game works entirely on your device and does not send any information to us or to anyone else.

No account, no personal information

CATNAP does not ask you to sign in or create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your progress and settings: which levels you have finished, story scenes you have seen, hints left, and your sound, music and vibration settings.
  • Local play statistics (test builds): during testing the game keeps simple gameplay statistics on your device, such as how long a level took, how many moves or hints were used, and when a play session started and ended. These files contain a random number created by the game to tell sessions apart; it is not linked to you, your device or any account.

These files are kept in the game's own storage on your phone. The game cannot send them anywhere: it does not have permission to use the internet. Uninstalling CATNAP deletes them.

If you have turned on Android's own backup to your Google account, Android may include the game's files in that backup so your progress can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android under your settings; we do not receive it.

What the game does not do

  • No internet connection: the app does not request the Android internet permission.
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs; the advertising ID is not used.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No Google sign-in and no Google Play Games services.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or file access.

The Full Journey purchase

Sunny Meadow is free. An optional one-time purchase, the Full Journey, opens the later maps. Purchases are made and paid entirely through Google Play, under Google's terms and privacy policy; we never see your name, payment details or Google account. To remember that you own the Full Journey (also offline, after a reinstall, or on a new phone), the game asks Google Play which purchases your account has and keeps a small note of it, together with Google Play's order reference, in the game's own storage on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Permissions

CATNAP uses vibration, for short, gentle haptic taps when you pick up or place a cat (you can switch these off in the game's Settings), and Google Play's billing permission, which lets it offer the Full Journey purchase through Google Play.

Children

CATNAP does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of CATNAP changes what it stores or starts using an online service, we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.
